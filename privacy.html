Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, with rclone, on the owner's own computer.

  • It requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file: access to files that it creates in the owner's Drive.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any user data. Nothing is sent to anyone other than Google.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only in the local rclone configuration on the owner's computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
